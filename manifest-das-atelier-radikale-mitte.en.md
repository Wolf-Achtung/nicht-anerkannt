# The Atelier of the Radical Middle
## A manifesto for young people who don't fit into camps and still want to act

*English translation of the founding manifesto. The German original — the historical document of this place — remains authoritative.*

**State-NOT accredited. Politically NOT co-optable.**  
**That is not a shortcoming. That is the promise.**

You don't need more material.  
You need a different operating system.

We live in a time when everything is simultaneously available: knowledge, opinions, outrage, tutorials, ideologies, identities, AI output, life plans. Everything is there. Everything speaks. Everything demands attention. And almost nothing helps you truly discern.

The old forms of education often act as if the problem were still a lack of information. As if you just needed enough material, enough exams, enough certificates, enough content. But that era is over. The real problem is not that we know too little. The real problem is that we live amidst too much material and have too few places where it becomes judgment, conviction, and action.

School and university still largely train reproduction. Restating. Categorising. Citing. Passing. Precision within the frame of other people's questions. That was already too little before. In a world with AI it becomes definitively barren. Because everything that is merely standardised, repeatable, and formattable becomes cheap. Very cheap.

Reproduction becomes cheap.  
Judgment, originality, taste, responsibility, presence, collaboration, and courage become expensive.

So the question is no longer: What do you know?  
The question is: What can you distinguish?  
What can you stand up for?  
What can you bring into the world with others when things become unclear?

## The False Alternative

Young people today are often presented with a false choice.

Either conformity: résumé optimisation, competence rhetoric, functional compliance, polished self-presentation, becoming marketable as early as possible.

Or camp thinking: moral overheating, ideological unambiguity, an identity built for outrage, reflexive enemy images, the comfortable relief of no longer having to think for yourself.

Both are insufficient.

Conformity makes you useful, but not alive.  
The camp makes you passionate, but not free.

Between the extremes lies not simply the lukewarm middle. There is something else: a stance that neither flees into a camp nor sinks into exhaustion. A stance that endures contradiction, takes reality seriously, and acts nonetheless.

We call this the **Radical Middle**.

Not the tired middle of administration.  
Not the dull somewhere-in-between.  
Not the cowardly equidistance between truth and lies.

But a middle that is radical because it must think where others merely react. Because it must connect where others draw lines. Because it must judge where others merely send signals. Because it must act although complete certainty never arrives.

Between democracy and authoritarianism there is no reasonable middle.  
Between dignity and contempt there is no reasonable middle.  
Between reality and lies there is no reasonable middle.

The Radical Middle does not mediate between everything. It distinguishes. And precisely because of that, it can connect.

## Why the Atelier

The Atelier is not a place that tells you what to think. It is a place where you learn to think better, question deeper, and act more decisively.

We do not believe that knowledge is simply transferred. Knowledge arises in the act of thinking, in the resistance of the material, in dispute with others, in failing, in attempting, in making, in revising.

Every real atelier knows this.

You don't learn form from definitions, but by working with something that talks back. You don't learn judgment from slides, but where decisions have consequences. You don't learn conviction through assertion, but when a situation becomes unclear and you must remain present anyway.

That is why the Atelier does not begin with ready-made answers. It begins with questions. With real questions. With questions no one in the room can immediately resolve.

Not: What must I master in order to pass?  
But: Which question is so real that it changes me if I engage with it seriously?

## How We Learn

We learn across disciplines, because all real problems cross disciplines.

Climate change is not just natural science. AI is not just technology. Democracy is not just constitutional law. Loneliness is not just psychology. The important problems of our time are **wicked problems**: complex, contradictory, not cleanly solvable, not lockable into a single subject.

That is why we do not work in subject boxes, but on real tensions.

We work in groups, because thinking is social. Not as a feel-good gathering, but as friction. In the group it becomes visible what holds, what is mere posture, what was asserted too quickly, what is still unthought.

We work with AI, but not as an oracle. The AI is a sparring partner, an amplifier, a counter-voice, a stress test. It delivers no final truth. It helps you sharpen your thoughts, take counter-arguments seriously, find dead ends faster, and open new perspectives.

Those who use AI only to produce output become replaceable.  
Those who use AI to think better become dangerous to every form of intellectual inertia.

We work playfully, but not playfully in the sense of arbitrariness. Play is not the opposite of seriousness. Play is the space in which the new can arise. The space in which you try, risk, fail, rearrange, think further. Without this space no real creativity emerges — only obedient variation.

We work publicly wherever possible. Not just for grades, not just for internal assessment, not just for the wastepaper basket. Real questions, real audiences, real consequences.

## What Emerges Here

The Atelier does not train opinion carriers.

The Atelier trains people who can think and act under uncertainty.

People

- who don't immediately fall into a camp when things get complicated,
- who can endure contradiction without going soft,
- who can stay open without becoming arbitrary,
- who can work with others without giving themselves up,
- who can use technology without being used by it,
- who don't merely comment on real problems, but work on them.

What emerges here is not mere competence. It is the power of judgment. The capacity to meet the present. Self-efficacy. Steadfastness. Collaboration. The will to give form. Responsibility.

Not for the exam. For reality.

## Who This Is For

The Atelier is not for everyone.

Not out of elitism, but out of honesty.

It is not for people who only seek confirmation. Not for people who only want another certificate. Not for people who demand certainty before thinking. Not for people who confuse utility with education.

It is for people who have long sensed that the prefabricated opinion is too small. For people who suspect that many debates are just poorly disguised repetition. For people who don't want to choose between cynicism and fanaticism. For people who want to be neither slickly conformist nor hysterically unambiguous.

It is for people who wish not merely for a career, but for effectiveness. Not merely for connection, but for resonance. Not merely for information, but for form.

For people who want to learn how not to become cynical in a damaged present.

## What This Is Not

Not esoteric.  
Not anti-academic.  
Not neutralist.  
Not feel-good education.  
Not brand-building with critical vocabulary.  
Not the next institution that administers young people.

The Atelier is a workroom for judgment, contradiction, play, conviction, and action.

## The Promise

We promise you no certainty.

We promise you no ideology in which all questions disappear.  
We promise you no degree that automatically saves you.  
We promise you no smooth story about the future.

We promise you something more difficult and more valuable:

A place where you can learn

- to think clearly when everything gets loud,
- to remain present when others tip into reflexes,
- to build something real with others,
- to let AI sharpen you without submitting to it,
- to find questions that are worth more than premature answers,
- and to become so capable of acting that you don't merely comment, but intervene.

## The Sentence

**Not more material. More judgment.**
