Why "for you and me"?

Because thinking doesn't end with graduation — and outsourcing doesn't begin with it.

The tools here don't grade and don't deliver. They push back, ask further, and show what you overlook. First you, then the AI — that holds for fourteen-year-olds and forty-year-olds alike.
